Design system · components and motion

Built from hairlines, not cards

Every component below is plain HTML plus the classes in gs.css. No default card. The only containers are the bottom sheet, the toast and, once per screen at most, the offset card.

Buttons

Primary .btn-primary: pencil yellow, graphite text, 1.5 px graphite edge and a hard 3 px under-edge, 56 px tall. One per screen, at the bottom, in thumb reach. Pressing it drops 2 px onto its edge.

Secondary .btn-secondary: graphite outline, 44 px. Quiet .btn-quiet: text with a hairline underline, still a 44 px target, for “Not tonight”, “Undo”, “Retake photo”.

Danger .btn-danger: the only red fill in the product. Reserved for Call Poison Help. .btn-danger-line is the outline form for Call 911. No other action, however destructive, may use them.

Chips

.chip with aria-pressed. 44 px tall, pill, 1.5 px control edge. Selected is the same yellow-and-graphite as a primary button, minus the weight, so “selected” and “the main thing” share one idea. Used for filters (To do, Done) and the child switcher.

List rows

.row: 64 px minimum, 1 px hairline below, no container, no icon tile, no chevron. The title is 17/25 at weight 500, the sub-line 13/18 muted. A row that opens something ends in a progress mark or a quiet link word (“Look up”), never an arrow. Room names use the display face (.d3) because rooms are places, not settings.

Task row and check

.task + .check. The hit area is 44 px (56 in easy mode) while the drawn box is 28 px. Unticked: a 1.5 px control-edge box. Ticked: graphite edge, a graphite pencil tick, and the offset fill shadow (3 px right, 3 px down) behind the box. That echo of the drawings is the only decoration. Done titles drop to --muted; they are never struck through, because crossing out reads as “wrong”.

Use role="checkbox" on a real <button> with aria-checked and a label that says what ticking does: “Mark Anchor the dresser as done”.

Progress mark

.pm: a jamb with ticks that climb from the bottom. Ticks are CSS, not images, so one tick per task is free. Lit ticks are graphite and tilted a hair; the next tick is yellow with a graphite ring; the rest are faint.

Two rules. In a room, one tick per task. For the whole home, always 12 ticks, lit = round(done ÷ total × 12), so 9 of 24 shows 5 lit and the 6th yellow. The number is always shown beside it: the mark is warmth, the figure is truth.

Bottom tab bar

.tabs: four tabs, Today, Rooms, Help, People. 56 px of bar plus 34 px home-indicator safe area. Icons are 24 px, 1.8 px stroke, drawn in the same round-cap graphite as the mark. The current tab gets a yellow tick above the icon and bold label; that tick is the whole “selected” treatment, so there is no tinted pill.

Labels are always shown. Tab labels never truncate: in easy mode they grow to 15 px and still fit at 375.

Bottom sheet and toast

Sheet .sheet: --surface, 24 px top radius, a 1.5 px graphite top edge, 40 × 4 grabber. Opens to 88% height, drags to dismiss, scrim at 42% graphite. The primary action is pinned at the bottom of the sheet, above the safe area.

Toast .toast: inverse ink on paper, 48 px, sits 8 px above the tab bar. Says what happened in plain words and offers Undo for 6 seconds. It never covers the primary action.

Motion

Motion is pencil on paper: a stroke being drawn, a frame being shaded in. Nothing bounces, nothing overshoots, and nothing runs longer than 400 ms. Haptics: one light tap on tick, one soft double tap when a room completes.

Reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)

Nothing draws or slides. The tick, offset fill and frame fill appear in their final state with a 120 ms opacity cross-fade. Sheet and toast fade in place with no translation. Haptics stay. The same CSS ships the rule:

.anim-draw  { animation: gs-draw var(--dur-tick) var(--ease-pencil) both; }
.anim-fill  { animation: gs-fill var(--dur-fill) cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) both; }
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  .anim-draw, .anim-fill { animation: gs-fade 120ms linear both; }
  .check .tk { transition: none; }
}

The same parts in dark

Dark mode is warm graphite, not black. The yellow does not change: it is the one colour that is already right on both grounds, which is why it is the signature. Ink and hairlines invert; the offset fill becomes a deep olive-gold that keeps the graphite line legible (7.2 : 1).
