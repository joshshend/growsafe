GrowSafe

A free, no-account web app that helps parents, grandparents and sitters make a home safe for a baby or toddler. Calm, competent, warm. Built from one idea: the pencil marks on a doorframe.

Logo and app icon

The mark is a doorframe jamb with three hand-tilted pencil ticks, each one longer than the last. It is the only thing in the product that is allowed to be called a logo. No shield, heart, leaf, house or padlock.

App icon · 1024 × 1024, full-bleed square (iOS applies the mask)

On pencil yellow the mark is all graphite, #24221E, with no offset fill. The yellow is the icon; recognition on a crowded home screen comes from colour first and the ticks second.

Mark on its three grounds

How red is kept honest

--emergency is allowed on two things: the Call Poison Help button and the Call 911 line (Help tab, Visit pack). Nothing else may be red, including errors, deletes, overdue and "not done". An error is a dashed ink border plus words. A hazard in a drawing is circled in graphite. When red appears, it means call someone.

Yellow is never used for text or for a boundary on paper (it is 1.6 : 1 against Paper). Yellow fills carry graphite text and a graphite 1.5 px edge; yellow as readable text uses --action-ink.

Done is graphite, not green

A finished fix is a pencil tick in --done. There is no success green and no warning orange: ticks, the offset yellow shadow behind a ticked box, and a calm muted label are the whole vocabulary. A parent at 11 pm never has to decode a colour.

Why this looks like GrowSafe and nothing else

GrowSafe borrows the one object every family already owns, a pencil mark on a doorframe, so its logo is a jamb with three hand-tilted ticks and its progress meter is those same ticks climbing. Its colour is pencil yellow, graphite and warm paper, which no baby, safety or health app uses, with red held back for Poison Help and 911 alone. Young Serif gives numbers and headlines the weight of a name written on a wall, while Atkinson Hyperlegible Next keeps instructions readable at 11 pm or at 70. Drawings are single-weight pencil with one flat yellow fill knocked slightly off-register, and that same offset reappears in ticked boxes and the one card, so everything reads as one hand. Structure comes from hairlines, whitespace and type size instead of tinted icon squares and chevrons, so no screen looks like a template.
