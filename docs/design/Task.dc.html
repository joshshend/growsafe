Anchor the TV

Why it matters

Children climb, and a TV pulled from the front can fall forward. A strap to the wall holds it in place.

What you need

A TV anchor strap kit, a screwdriver, and a stud finder if you have one.

Steps
1Pick a spot on the wall behind the TV that can hold a screw. A stud is best.
2Fix one end of the strap to the back of the TV.
3Fix the other end to the wall and pull the strap snug.
4Give the TV a gentle tug. It should not move.
