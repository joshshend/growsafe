# GrowSafe demo video: 2:30 max, recorded on the iPhone from the home-screen app

Rules: your own voice, captions burned in or uploaded as a caption file, no music over speech, no claims you can't back up on screen. Record screen + voice in 4 to 6 takes, one per scene, then cut together. Use the example family so nothing real appears. Rehearse once; read the text below as a guide, not word for word.

0:00-0:15 The problem (voice over a shot of the Today screen)
"Every year, a lot of children get hurt at home by things a parent could have fixed in an hour, and the advice is scattered across pages written for nobody in particular. GrowSafe turns your child's age and your home into one ranked to-do list." (Do not add a statistic unless you have opened its source; the CPSC tip-over figure from the KB is safe to cite with its source on screen: about 17,800 ER-treated tip-over injuries a year, 2020 to 2022. Re-check it first.)

0:15-0:40 Set up in three taps
Welcome > Get started > child nickname and birth month > places (my home, grandparents') > rooms > "Looks right". Land on Today with the plan built. Say: "No account, no sign-up. Everything stays on this phone."

0:40-1:05 Why this order
Open the hero task. Show steps, time, cost, then "Why this matters" and the source link opening the CPSC or CDC page. Say: "Every tip links to its source. The order comes from how serious each hazard is for this child's age."

1:05-1:30 Plan my weekend
Tap Plan a safety session > 90 minutes, $50 > Make my plan. Show the chosen tasks, supplies list, total cost, Add to calendar. Say: "I have 90 minutes and $50. It picks the changes that matter most within that and gives me a shopping list."

1:30-1:50 What's coming
Open the growth preview, drag the slider from 9 to 15 months. Say: "Drag forward and the plan changes before the risk arrives: pulling up to stand, then climbing."

1:50-2:10 Grandparents and sitters
People > Send a visit pack > show the link. Switch to the helper view (second device or window): tick two items, Send back. Back on the parent phone: "Grandma finished 2 tasks" > Confirm. Say: "No accounts. The link carries the plan, and the reply link carries what they did."

2:10-2:25 Everyone can use it
Settings > Easy mode (big buttons, one task at a time), tap Read to me, flip to Spanish. Say: "Large text, read aloud, and Spanish, because the people watching the baby aren't always the ones who set up the app."

2:25-2:30 Close
Poison Help button and Help tab. Say: "GrowSafe. Free, no ads, no accounts." Your name and district on the last frame.

## Before you record

- Install the deployed app to the Home Screen; use the example family; turn on Do Not Disturb and hide notifications.
- Rehearse the visit-pack round trip twice; have the second device ready (iPad, a laptop browser, or a private window).
- Check every number you say against its source on screen. Cut anything you can't show.
- If the guided room check (Room check, /scan) is built and works, add it at 1:30 in place of 10 seconds of the weekend scene.
