- 2026-10-24 Hazards are only assumed after the person checks a room. A new room is "Not checked yet" with no tasks; tasks come from the tap list (onboarding step 4 or the Room page), accepted photo-scan results or gear checklists. Rooms store `checks[room] = {checkedAt, cues}` on the home; re-check shows as due after 30 days. The plan engine and knowledge base are unchanged; the store only keeps tasks the person confirmed. Saves from before this change lose their untouched to-do tasks on load; anything done, doing, owned, dated or from a gear checklist stays. The sample family is pre-checked.
- 2026-10-24 Height tracker ("Mia's doorframe", /height): records and draws height marks only. No percentiles, no normal/abnormal, no growth advice, because any of those is a medical claim; the only maths is "grew X since <date>", plus "For your own records. Ask your child's doctor about growth." Marks live in their own IndexedDB key (growsafe:heights), never in share links or plan files, are pruned when a child is removed, and are cleared by Erase data. Stored in cm (2 to 200), unit toggle remembered.
- 2026-10-24 Check-in reminders (/reminders, linked from Settings and Today): no push notifications, because real push needs accounts and a server and GrowSafe has neither. Instead the screen downloads a calendar (.ics) file with repeating events (weekly check-in, monthly room re-checks, plan updates at age milestones) and the person's own calendar app does the reminding. Times are floating local times (no TZID) so 9:00 stays 9:00 when travelling. Events have fixed UIDs so adding the file again updates rather than duplicates. Choices are not saved; nothing is sent anywhere.
- 2026-10-24 Photo scan draws boxes on the person's own photo. The model still only reports cues from the allowed list; it may add an optional `box` ([ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax], integers 0-1000). The proxy and the app both drop an invalid box (wrong shape, out of range, empty, or over 90% of the image) and keep the finding; findings without a box stay as plain rows. The photo is shown from an in-memory object URL on the review step only, revoked when replaced, cleared or unmounted, never stored and never sent anywhere new. Boxes are a pointer, not a verdict: they carry no advice and no risk wording.
- 2026-10-24 Rooms opens with a Home map, not just a list: a pencil cutaway of a two-storey house where each room is a real link (aria-label like "Nursery, 3 of 6 done") and its fill is its state: hatch = not checked, pencil yellow (stronger with more and more serious open fixes, severity-weighted, never red) = fixes open with a count badge, graphite tick = done. Rooms the home lacks are drawn dashed and dim and are not links; yard, car and garage appear under the house only if the home has them. The list below stays as the keyboard-first way in. Legend wording avoids "Not checked" so it does not collide with other screens' text.
- 2026-10-24 "One step ahead": the central pitch is now "a safety plan that stays one step ahead of your child". Today opens with a short "Coming up for <name>" strip above "Right now": the next up to 3 CDC checkpoints within 12 months (from src/kb/stages.json) and, under each, the KB hazards whose risk starts after the previous line and by that checkpoint (nextUnlocks, so it matches /ahead). The pairing is by age only, not a claim that a milestone causes a hazard; lines with no new hazards show the milestone alone. Hidden in easy mode and for children past 5 years. The old "Coming up at <age>" task disclosure is renamed "Tasks added early" and the 18-month link moved to the strip.
- 2026-10-24 stages.json re-checked against the CDC milestone pages (2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 months, 2, 30 months, 3, 4, 5 years; each "last reviewed May 16, 2026") through WebFetch summaries, not by a person. Only movement and cognitive items were checked; wording that CDC does not list (looking at faces, swinging and climbing, wanting to do things alone, running and kicking at 12-24 months) was removed. Stages are now "sourced"; each checkpoint carries its own CDC page. Spanish is machine translation, unreviewed.
- 2026-10-24 Gear shelf: each product has one status line (No recall found with date / Possible recall with the CPSC link / Not checked yet / Couldn't check right now) and "Check all" runs the existing check one item at a time (15 s timeout each, failures never stop the rest). Car seats and boosters get an optional typed Expires date and an Expiry status; the app never knows a seat's real expiry rule. Car seat recalls are published by NHTSA, so seats also link to nhtsa.gov/recalls, and the CPSC search on a seat can miss them. The CPSC recall lookup has not been tested against the live service. No FDA.
